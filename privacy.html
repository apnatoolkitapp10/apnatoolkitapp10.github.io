Apna Toolkit – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Apna Toolkit ("the app") is a set of photo, PDF and calculator tools for Android. This policy explains what information is used when you use the app.

1. Your photos, documents and numbers stay on your phone

All photo, PDF and calculator tools run on your device. Your photos, scans, PDFs, names, dates and the numbers you type are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Files you create are saved in your phone's Pictures/ApnaToolkit or Download/ApnaToolkit folder. We do not operate servers that receive your files.

2. Google services used on your device

  • The document scanner, background remover and QR scanner use Google ML Kit through Google Play services. They process images on your device. ML Kit may send Google basic diagnostic information (device model, Android and app version, performance and error codes, a per-installation identifier) to keep these features working; your images are not sent.
  • Google Play may show in-app review and in-app update prompts.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (views and clicks) and diagnostics.

Google uses this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message and can change their choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices inside the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

4. Information stored on your phone

The app remembers your language, favourite tools, the list of files you created and a few counters (for example, how many tasks you finished, so ads are not shown too often). This stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

5. Online settings

The app may download a small public settings file (for example how often ads are shown) from this website. No personal information is sent, apart from the normal technical data any web request includes (such as IP address).

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and the settings file.
  • Storage (only Android 9 and older): to save your files into Pictures and Download.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone, call logs or all your photos. You choose each photo through Android's photo picker.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Security

Because your files are processed on your device and never uploaded, they are not exposed on the internet by the app. Files you choose to share with other apps are handled by those apps.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not store your data on any server, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Everything the app stores stays on your phone and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it; files you saved in Pictures or Download stay until you delete them. Data collected by Google for ads and diagnostics is kept and deleted under Google's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings at any time.

10. Not a government app

Apna Toolkit is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government body, exam board or bank. It does not provide government information or services.

11. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the app with a new effective date. For questions about this policy or your data, email apnatoolkitapp@gmail.com.
